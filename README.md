Park Mate – Smart City Parking Solution
Project Overview
Park Mate is a proposed smart parking mobile application designed to improve the parking experience in Australian metropolitan areas, with a focus on challenges faced by drivers in cities such as Newcastle and Sydney. The concept aims to help users find available parking, understand pricing, reserve spaces, and make digital payments through one integrated platform.

This project was completed for BUSA3003 – Business Process Modelling at the University of Newcastle.
Problem Statement
Drivers can face unclear parking prices, fragmented parking systems, unreliable availability information, and time-consuming payment processes. These issues can contribute to frustration, unnecessary driving, traffic congestion, and reduced parking-management efficiency.
Proposed Solution
Park Mate proposes an integrated smart parking platform combining:
- Real-time parking information to show available, reserved, and occupied spaces.
- Pre-booking and transparent pricing to help users compare options and reserve parking.
- Automated entry and exit using License Plate Recognition (LPR) technology.
- Digital payments through supported cashless payment methods.
- AI-based predictive analytics to estimate parking demand and availability.
- IoT sensor integration to support live parking updates.
- Accessibility filters for features such as wheelchair-accessible spaces and EV charging.
- Notifications, booking history, favourites, and booking extensions to support the user journey.
- 24/7 chatbot assistance and a rewards programme to improve customer support and engagement.
Methodology
The report applies three stages of the Design Thinking framework:
1. Empathize – Consider the experiences and pain points of drivers, including students, commuters, seniors, people with disabilities, and delivery drivers.
2. Define – Frame the core challenge: drivers need affordable, accessible, and reliable real-time parking information because current systems can be inconsistent and disconnected.
3. Ideate – Explore possible features using a LOTUS Blossom Diagram, including a real-time parking map, booking, chatbot support, predictive analytics, price transparency, accessibility, payment integration, and rewards.
Business Process Modelling
The report compares the existing AS-IS off-street parking process with the proposed TO-BE Park Mate process.
- AS-IS: Drivers enter using a ticket or LPR, park, pay at a pay station, and have their payment checked at the exit. Failed payments and lost tickets may require staff assistance.
- TO-BE: Drivers use the app to find and reserve a space. LPR verifies the reservation at arrival, parking sessions and pricing are updated by the backend, and digital payment is collected when the driver exits.
The proposed process is intended to reduce manual steps and improve the integration of parking, payment, and operational data.
Business Model
Potential revenue streams discussed in the report include:
- Booking and transaction service fees
- User subscription plans and premium features
- Advertising and promoted listings
- Commissions from parking providers
- Partnerships with nearby businesses
The report also identifies privacy and ethical considerations associated with sharing or commercialising parking-related data.
Expected Benefits
- Drivers: Easier parking searches, clearer pricing, convenient booking and payment, and improved accessibility.
- Parking operators: Potentially improved occupancy, pricing management, and revenue performance.
- Local councils: Data insights to support parking infrastructure planning, traffic management, and sustainability initiatives.
Recommendations
The report recommends building partnerships with local councils and private parking operators, improving AI-based availability predictions, and collecting user feedback to continuously improve usability and accessibility.
Deliverable
The accompanying report documents the project rationale, Design Thinking method, AS-IS and TO-BE process models, proposed business model, prototype features, stakeholder benefits, recommendations, and references.
